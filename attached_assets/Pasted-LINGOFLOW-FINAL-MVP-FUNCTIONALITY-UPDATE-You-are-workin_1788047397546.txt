PART 3 — PART OF SPEECH CLASSIFICATION
==================================================

The AI must classify each word.

Supported categories:

- Noun — اسم
- Verb — فعل
- Adjective — صفة
- Adverb — ظرف
- Pronoun — ضمير
- Preposition — حرف جر
- Conjunction — أداة ربط
- Article / Determiner — أداة تعريف/تحديد
- Expression — تعبير
- Other — أخرى

IMPORTANT:

A word can have multiple grammatical uses.

For example:

"light"

can be:

- Noun
- Verb
- Adjective

Do not incorrectly force every word into one category when the word genuinely has multiple grammatical functions.

If necessary, store:

primaryPartOfSpeech
and
alternativePartsOfSpeech

The AI should select the most appropriate classification for the intended learning context.
